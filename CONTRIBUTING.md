# Contributing

Thank you for your interest.

## Issues are welcome

You can use [issues](../../issues) to report bugs, point out problems in the documentation, or suggest improvements. Please search the existing issues first.

- **Bugs:** use the bug report form.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open a public issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

An issue may not get a quick reply, and not every suggestion will be accepted.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are not open to everyone at the moment. If you have something you would like to contribute, feel free to get in touch.

You are free to fork the project and change your copy under the terms of the [MIT license](LICENSE).

## What you post

Issues are public. Remove personal data, passwords, tokens, and private paths from text, logs, screenshots, and attachments.

Only submit material that you have the right to share. If you submit code, documentation, or a suggested fix, whether in an issue or a pull request, you agree that it may be used in GLIRPS under the MIT license. Do not submit material copied from a project with an incompatible license.
